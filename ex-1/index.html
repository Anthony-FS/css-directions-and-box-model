France

France is a country in Western Europe. It is known for its rich history, culture, and cuisine. The capital city of France is Paris, which is famous for its iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre Museum.

Italy

Italy is a country in Southern Europe. It is known for its rich history, culture, and cuisine. The capital city of Italy is Rome, which is famous for its iconic landmarks such as the Colosseum and the Vatican City.
